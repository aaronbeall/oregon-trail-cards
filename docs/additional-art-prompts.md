# Additional generated artwork

Generated using the built-in image generation tool. Optimized WebP assets are in `../assets/`. Portraits form a 3×2 atlas; new parties randomly deal unique portrait slots and save those assignments with the journey.

## town.webp

Use case: illustration-story. Original raster illustration for a minimal Oregon Trail card game. Flat vintage 1940s travel poster, hand-cut linocut, distressed screen printing on cream paper. Soothing sage green, faded teal, ochre, dusty terracotta and charcoal olive inks, fine grain, ink dropout and worn edges. No gradients, glossy rendering, typography, borders or watermark. Landscape 3:2. A tiny welcoming 1847 frontier town: timber storefronts, a porch and hitching post, quiet dirt street, hills behind. No close-up people. Simple legible silhouettes for a small illustrated game card.
