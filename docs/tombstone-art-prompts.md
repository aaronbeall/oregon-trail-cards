# Tombstone templates

Generated with built-in imagegen with transparent backgrounds. Originals remain in the thread’s generated_images directory.

- `assets/tombstone-full.webp` (600×800): front-facing rounded arched gravestone with chipped edge and broad base; distressed vintage linocut on pale warm-gray stone, charcoal olive outline, cream highlights. Blank central face with subtle grain, no lettering or ornaments. R.I.P., player name, and editable epitaph remain live text over the template. Calamity artwork supplied as a style-only reference.
- `assets/tombstone-icon.webp` (128×128): simplified matching stone referenced from the full-size template, chunky outline and minimal detail for 28–44px display, one bold cross, no text or scenery. Used wherever a dead player replaces their portrait.

The assets preserve transparency; the UI adds no opacity fade to deceased players.
