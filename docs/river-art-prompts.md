# River artwork

Generated using built-in imagegen, with river-ford.webp as a style-only reference: flat distressed vintage linocut screenprint on cream paper, muted sage/ochre/terracotta/charcoal olive inks, heavy grain and ink dropout. Landscape 3:2, no text or frame. Exports are 900×600 WebP; original generated files remain in the thread’s generated_images directory.

- river-success.webp: intact wagon and oxen climbing onto dry grass on the far bank, river behind them; safe arrival.
- river-supply-loss.webp: overhead close-up of a crate, food sack, canteen and cloth swept away by rushing water; cropped wagon wheel on shore.
- river-drowning.webp: floating traveller’s hat and a sinking hand in swirling water, concerned companion and wagon on the distant shore; restrained and non-graphic.

The original river-ford.webp remains the unresolved crossing scene. River cards show a moderate crossing for supply-loss risk or a raging river for drowning risk and a “Risk” consequence tag. Result scenes distinguish success, supply loss and drowning; rolls of 3/5 retain the unresolved crossing artwork.

- river-moderate.webp: wagon and oxen fording a substantial flowing river, visible current and whitecaps, all travellers and supplies secure.
- river-raging.webp: deep swollen river and churning rapids dominate the scene; wagon and team wait safely on the rocky bank.

Crossing previews never show a failure before it happens. The existing gentle river-ford.webp illustrates harmless failed attempts; failure scenes are reserved for actual dice results and the ensuing supply-loss action.
