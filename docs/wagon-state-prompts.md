# Wagon-state artwork

Remade with built-in imagegen to give each failure a distinct, close-up composition. `assets/calamity.webp` was used as a style reference only and remains the generic unrevealed calamity illustration. Mechanical failures appear in repair panels and active hazards. All six shared calamities have distinct images in the wagon section, and an intact wagon appears when no wagon calamities are active.

## assets/broken-wheel.webp

Use case: illustration-story. Standalone landscape 3:2 raster illustration for an Oregon Trail card game, no text/frame/watermark. Attached image is STYLE reference ONLY; DO NOT copy its composition, wagon pose or scenery. Match flat vintage linocut/distressed screenprint on cream paper, muted sage/ochre/terracotta/charcoal olive inks, fine grain and ink dropout, soothing understated palette. The failure fills most of the frame and must read at thumbnail size. BROKEN WHEEL. Extreme close-up at ground level of ONE wooden wagon wheel with a large missing rim segment and two snapped spokes, jagged pale wood and pieces lying beside it. Wheel dominates foreground at a diagonal, hub and remaining spokes clearly legible. Only a small cropped corner of wagon bed above it, no whole covered wagon, no distant mesas or expansive landscape. A patch of dusty grass behind. Clear broken wheel, not a detached intact wheel.

## Additional wagon states

Generated with built-in imagegen using calamity.webp as a style-only reference: landscape 3:2, flat distressed vintage linocut screenprint on cream paper, sage/ochre/terracotta/charcoal olive inks, grain and ink dropout, no text or frame. Each source is preserved in the thread’s generated_images directory and exported to a 900×600 WebP.

- `assets/unsafe-water.webp`: overhead close-up of stagnant olive water, surface scum and a tipped wooden bucket on the muddy shore; no wagon or panorama.
- `assets/inadequate-grass.webp`: living ox lowering its head towards sparse brittle stalks on cracked parched grazing ground; no wagon or water.
- `assets/healthy-wagon.webp`: intact covered wagon and two healthy yoked oxen on a grassy trail, three-quarter front view.

## Travel direction

Trail artwork is oriented left-to-right. The finished broken-tongue, calamity, dead-oxen, healthy-wagon, river-drowning, river-ford, river-moderate and river-raging images were mirrored horizontally after generation. Portraits and scenery without a directional traveller were retained. Future travelling wagon/team scenes should face right.
